Our Mission

We believe in making things simple and fun. Our goal is to build tools that help people learn, collaborate, and create — without getting in the way. Whether you're just getting started or have been at it for years, we want to make your experience a little better every day.

Our Values

  • Simplicity — Keep things clear and easy to understand.
  • Curiosity — Never stop asking questions and exploring.
  • Collaboration — Great things are built together.
  • Fun — If it's not enjoyable, we're doing it wrong.
